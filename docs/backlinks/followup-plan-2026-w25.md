# Backlink Follow-Up Plan - 2026-W25

## Status Rules

- `prospect`: target found, not sent.
- `sent`: first email sent.
- `followup_1_sent`: first follow-up sent after 7 days.
- `followup_2_sent`: second follow-up sent after 14 days.
- `replied`: any human reply.
- `published`: live backlink or mention found.
- `declined`: explicit no.
- `closed_no_reply`: no reply after second follow-up plus 7 days.

## Do Not Do

- Do not send all 30 at once.
- Do not use fake personalization.
- Do not ask directly for "SEO backlinks" in the email.
- Do not follow up more than twice.
- Do not send to unrelated editorial teams if a contact page says no pitches.

## Weekly Review Checklist

Every Tuesday:

- Update `stage`.
- Add sent timestamps.
- Add any replies.
- Search for live mentions.
- Move live links to `published_url`.
- Mark link type as `follow`, `nofollow`, `ugc`, `sponsored`, or `unknown`.
