# Queer Atlas Risk Index (QARI) · Country expansion v1.1

Status: Published country baseline dataset  
Review date: 2026-08-22  
Scope: All 81 countries and territories represented by Queer Atlas destinations

## What changed after the eight-country pilot

Version 1.1 retains the pilot formula, bands and eight manually reviewed profiles. It adds a reproducible, fully sourced baseline for the other 73 Atlas countries. No venue count, event count, review count or other measure of Queer Atlas content affects a score.

## Formula

`QARI = (Legal risk × 0.35) + (Social reality × 0.40) + (Digital & enforcement risk × 0.25)`

Each axis is scored from 0 to 100, where a higher number means higher documented risk. The weighted result is rounded to a whole number. A verified risk floor can raise, but never lower, the result.

The map and QARI panels use one five-level classification formula across the complete score range: **0–20 Lower risk** (blue), **21–40 Lower context** (green), **41–60 Moderate** (yellow), **61–80 High risk** (orange) and **81–100 Extreme high risk** (red). The QARI score directly determines the map and panel colour; the underlying weighted score remains unchanged.

## Legal risk · 35%

Legal risk is the inverse of the available multi-source legal-rights composite. Each country requires at least two inputs from:

- F&M Global Barometers · Global Barometer of Gay Rights;
- Equaldex Equality Index · legal score;
- ILGA-Europe Rainbow Map 2026, for covered European countries.

This axis covers criminalisation, equality protection, family recognition, identity recognition and related legal-policy conditions. ILGA World criminalisation data is additionally used when a QARI risk floor applies.

## Social reality · 40%

Social risk is the inverse of the available multi-source lived-experience composite. Each country requires at least two inputs from:

- F&M Global Barometers · Global Barometer of Gay Rights, lived-experience dimension;
- Equaldex Equality Index · public-opinion score;
- Williams Institute · Global Acceptance Index.

These inputs measure national acceptance and lived conditions. They do not claim that every neighbourhood, identity or traveller has the same experience.

## Digital and enforcement risk · 25%

Where available, this axis combines:

- 75% inverse Freedom House · Freedom on the Net 2025 score; and
- 25% QARI LGBTQ+ legal-risk score.

Freedom on the Net covers 72 countries worldwide and measures obstacles to access, limits on content and violations of user rights. Where it has no country score, version 1.1 uses the inverse Freedom in the World 2026 score as a clearly labelled civic-freedom proxy, again blended 75/25 with LGBTQ+ legal risk. A proxy is not presented as direct evidence of dating-app entrapment or an LGBTQ+-specific digital incident.

Puerto Rico uses the United States Freedom House score only for this digital/civic proxy; its legal and social inputs remain Puerto Rico-specific. The source label exposes that substitution.

## Floors and publication

The v1.0 floor rules remain unchanged. In the generated expansion, ILGA World criminalisation evidence applies a floor to Indonesia, Lebanon, Malaysia and Morocco. The eight pilot profiles retain their original editorial findings and floors.

Every published country profile contains all three numerical axes and at least one source row per axis. Generated profiles are marked as `Queer Atlas multi-source model`, not falsely as human-reviewed editorial profiles. Confidence is:

- **High** only when Freedom on the Net is available and both legal and social composites each contain three sources;
- **Medium** for complete, publishable profiles that use two-source composites or the disclosed Freedom in the World proxy;
- **Low** profiles are not published.

## Limits

QARI is a country-level travel-planning baseline, not a promise of safety. National averages can hide major city, regional and identity-specific differences. The dataset must be dated, sources must remain visible and material legal changes require regeneration or editorial correction.
